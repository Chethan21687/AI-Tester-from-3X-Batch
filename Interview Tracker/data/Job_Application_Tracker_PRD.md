# Job Application Tracker System — Product Requirements Document

## Introduction
A web-based recruitment platform for candidates, recruiters, hiring managers, company administrators and HR administrators to manage the end-to-end hiring lifecycle.

## Business Objectives
Reduce hiring time, improve recruiter productivity, centralize candidate data, automate recruitment workflows, improve candidate experience, and provide hiring analytics.

## User Roles
Candidate, Recruiter, Hiring Manager, HR Administrator, Company Administrator.

## Core Features
Authentication, Candidate Profile, Resume Upload, Company Management, Job Posting, Job Search, Job Applications, Candidate Pipeline, Interview Scheduling, Interview Feedback, Offer Management, Notifications.

## AI Features
Resume Parsing, Resume Matching, AI Candidate Ranking, AI Interview Assistant, AI Job Recommendation.

## Reports
Hiring reports, recruitment funnel, recruiter performance, offer acceptance, interview statistics.

## Database Entities
Users, Roles, Companies, Recruiters, Candidates, Jobs, Applications, Interviews, Feedback, Offers, Notifications, Audit Logs.

## End-to-End Workflow
Company Registration → Recruiter Added → Job Created → Job Published → Candidate Registration → Resume Upload → Job Search → Apply → Resume Screening → AI Matching → Shortlisting → Technical Interview → Manager Interview → HR Interview → Offer Generation → Offer Acceptance → Background Verification → Employee Onboarding.

## Future Enhancements
AI chatbot, AI resume builder, salary prediction, video interview analysis, HRMS integration, LinkedIn/Indeed integration.
